Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? ▼

Navigate to the "Find Doctors" page using the link in the top menu or dashboard. Search for your preferred doctor, view their available time slots, fill in your symptoms, and click "Book slot". Complete the payment checkout to confirm your consultation slot.

What is the Wallet feature? ▼

The wallet allows you to load funds in advance. When checking out an appointment, you can instantly pay using your wallet balance for a much faster booking flow, without needing to verify cards during checkout.

How do I access my prescription? ▼

After a consultation is completed, the doctor will write and submit a prescription. Go to the "Prescriptions" tab on your sidebar menu. You will find a list of all prescriptions, where you can view medical instructions and print/download the page.

Can I cancel an appointment? ▼

Yes, you can cancel any confirmed appointment up to the scheduled consultation time. Go to "Upcoming Appointments" and click the cancel button. The slots will be immediately released. Please refer to our refund policy in the terms section.

How do support tickets work? ▼

If you run into payment issues, system bugs, or have other questions, you can open a new ticket in the "Support Tickets" section. Our administrative team will review it and reply directly inside the ticket portal.

Still need help?

Submit a support ticket and our customer care team will respond within 24 hours.
